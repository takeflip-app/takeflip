Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

TAKEFLIP is a satirical card game for iOS. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or visit this website.

1. Information collected by the app

TAKEFLIP does not require an account and does not collect personal information through its own systems. The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies, and it does not send your card choices or votes to a developer-operated server.

2. Votes and game activity

Your votes and game progress are handled locally on your device during the current session. They are not transmitted to us or stored on our servers. Session information may be lost when the app is closed or restarted.

3. Apple services

Apple may independently process information associated with downloading, installing, purchasing, or testing apps, including diagnostic information, under Apple's own terms and privacy policies. If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple's TestFlight service may collect crash reports, usage information, and feedback. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. This website

Our support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and request details, to deliver and protect the website. We do not use our own website analytics or advertising trackers. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your inquiry and handle support requests. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

6. Children

TAKEFLIP is not designed to collect information from children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

7. Changes

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email takeflip@web.de.
